# Vision

## One sentence

Kerix is the herald: you speak from wherever you are, and somewhere a machine starts working.

## Three horizons

**Horizon 1 · Capture.** A thought typed in two seconds on a phone is on the laptop before you sit down. It never asks you to wait for a network. This is a notebook that is always with you and never loses anything. Milestones 1 to 5 in [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md).

**Horizon 2 · Dispatch.** A thought becomes a task and is sent to a *daimon*: a GitHub Actions runner, a server, or the laptop at home. The daimon pulls the work, does it, and reports back into the thought's slab. The owner commands; the fleet executes. Issue #2.

**Horizon 3 · Counsel.** A local model on the device answers questions over everything you have ever captured, with nothing leaving the device. Issue #1.

## Who it is for

One person first: the owner, who moves between a phone, a laptop and a desk and thinks in fragments. Built so that it could become a product for others without rebuilding, which is why login, data ownership and sync are designed properly from the start.

## What it is not

- Not a project management tool. No boards, no assignees, no due-date calendars.
- Not a chat app. Dispatch is a command, not a conversation.
- Not a cloud service that happens to cache. The device is the source of truth; the server is a relay.

## Brand

Ancient Greek forms fused with Isu high technology, from *Assassin's Creed Odyssey: The Fate of Atlantis*. Two modes, Isu Marble by day and Nexus by night, from one set of tokens. The naming family: **Archon** rules, **Kerix** carries the command, **daimons** do the work.
